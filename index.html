Course plan

Our IELTS Academic preparation course is a focused 12-week online programme delivered on Zoom. Classes combine exam strategy, guided practice, language development and interactive activities. The course balances all four skills (Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking) while also working on grammar, vocabulary, paraphrasing and exam techniques.

Format
Live on Zoom
Small-group online classes
Target level
B1 to C1
For students preparing for the IELTS Academic exam
Class size
6 + 2 students
Each class reserves 2 places for students from Gaza
Required material
Official IELTS Academic practice tests book
